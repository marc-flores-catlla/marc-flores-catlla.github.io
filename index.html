Light Curve Transformer: classifying irregular astronomical time series

Research internship at Scalai · Supervisor: Dr. Vicens Gaitan · 9.9 / 10

A Transformer encoder that classifies light curves into 25 variability classes (supernovae, microlensing, variable stars…) directly from irregular observations (Δt, magnitude, error), with no interpolation to a regular grid. Because labelled data are scarce, the model is trained on 25 million synthetic light curves resampled with the cadences and photometric noise of real telescope observations, and then applied to real observatory data in real time. It is being integrated into Light Bridges' platform for astronomers, and I presented it to the company's CSO, Dr. Miquel Serra-Ricart.

  • Learnable time embedding (linear + sinusoidal components with learned frequencies) and masked mean pooling.
  • Noise model that samples photometric errors conditioned on magnitude and time gap.
  • Mixed-precision training with data-driven input clipping, evaluated on synthetic and labelled real catalogues.

Statistical Modelling of Inter-Event Times for Extreme Event Forecasting

BSc thesis in Mathematics, UAB · Advisor: Dr. Víctor Navas · 9.1 / 10

A framework for forecasting rare extreme events from the waiting times between threshold exceedances. Exponential, Weibull and Gamma models are compared, including a Gamma regression whose scale depends on the threshold. Applied to solar radio bursts at 1415 MHz (relevant to GNSS), it rejects the homogeneous Poisson model and estimates a ten-year probability of (18.8 ± 3.4)% for a burst above 106 SFU.

A publication of these results is under discussion with Dr. Víctor Navas and Dr. Moriña.

Modeling Skyrmions and 2π-Skyrmions in Magnetic Tracks for Ternary Logic Gates

BSc thesis in Physics, UAB · Advisor: Dr. Carles Navau · 9.2 / 10

Ternary logic built on magnetic textures: no object encodes 0, a skyrmion encodes 1 and a 2π-skyrmion encodes 2. Using micromagnetic simulations of the Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert equation, written in C, in nanotracks driven by spin Hall torque, I designed an OR/MAX gate and a two-stage AND/MIN gate. The AND/MIN gate reproduces the full ternary truth table in about 30 ns.
